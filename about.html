Backend Engineer — Cloud Commerce, B2B Platforms & Payment Integration

I've been building cloud commerce platforms since 2017 — spanning consumer-facing applications and multi-organization B2B management systems with hierarchical organization management, configurable RBAC (fixed & custom roles, menu and API permissions), data-scope access control, and per-brand configuration. I've developed new features across product and inventory modules as the business evolved, and designed and implemented core order and payment workflows — including an independently designed merchant payment allocation model — plus the financial reconciliation and reporting behind them.

Payment is my strongest technical area: payment state machines, webhook verification, idempotency, refunds, reconciliation, and compensation when third-party services misbehave. I've integrated WeChat Pay, Alipay+, Stripe, Nayax, and RevenueMonster into unified backend flows, and I connect retail devices — POS, vending machines, self-service terminals — from the cloud side through HTTPS APIs, collaborating with embedded engineers on the device link. I structure backends with DDD and hexagonal boundaries, work spec-first, and use AI-assisted coding to deliver faster without giving up engineering judgment.

I also ship products end-to-end on my own: MachineCat, an award-winning AI robotic companion where I built the entire software stack — the robot itself was an off-the-shelf kit brought by teammates, and I drove it through its exposed HTTP / WebSocket / Bluetooth interfaces — and TarotStars, a hackathon prototype with a Telegram Bot and Mini App for daily tarot drawing.
